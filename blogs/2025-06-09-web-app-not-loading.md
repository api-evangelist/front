Jun 9 , 21:00 UTC Resolved - A configuration change was made to the Content Distribution Network that caused the web clients to lose access to files that were needed for startup. The web app couldn't be loaded between 1:48 PM and 2:16 PM PDT. Impact was limited to users who tried to start the app between those times.
